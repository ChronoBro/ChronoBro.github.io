This project brings the pencil-and-paper space combat game I played as a kid to the screen. You can play against the computer or hop into an online match.

This is an ongoing prototype, and the game is still evolving. It works best on a desktop browser; touch controls are experimental.

[Play the prototype on itch.io](https://chronobro.itch.io/space-combat?password=itsflickintime)
